QUESTION 07. which states contribute the most patients?
Borno and Akwa Ibom states accounted for the most patients while the state with a slightly lower contribution of patients was Cross River. These values may not be entirely reliable since some personal information that patients provide such as State of origin cannot be verified by the hospital.

QUESTION 08. what is the average length of stay (in days) per department
Patients in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) stayed an average of 8 days while patients at the Oncology department spent an average of 5 days. Surprisingly for all other departments, patients only spent an average of 4 days. This differences in length of stay might be influenced by factors such as severity of a patient's case as well as some patients leaving against medical advice.
